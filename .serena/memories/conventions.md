HA conventions: async setup/unload through __init__.py; one shared DataUpdateCoordinator for all entries; options are merged over entry.data; sensor platform uses CoordinatorEntity; has_entity_name is enabled. Filter semantics: power unavailable values fail-soft to 0; energy/water fail-closed; optional invert, fallback_zero, and monotonic spike guard. Config flow accepts one or multiple source entity IDs and requires at least one. Do not assume entity unique IDs may change when source mappings change: current sensor.py derives them from source IDs, conflicting with README's stable-entity promise.
